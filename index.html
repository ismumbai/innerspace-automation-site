How It Works

Each workflow is planned around the business's requirements and configured with appropriate access and permissions.

  1. Understand the business: Identify enquiry types, customer needs and existing processes.
  2. Configure the workflow: Set up relevant responses, information collection and routing rules.
  3. Organise enquiries: Send relevant information to the business systems configured for the service.
  4. Support the team: Route enquiries for human attention when required.
  5. Review and improve: Refine the workflow based on agreed business requirements.
Human support remains important. Automated responses support the business team; staff can handle enquiries that require personal judgement or assistance.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

InnerSpace respects the privacy of customers and businesses using its automation services. This policy explains how information may be handled when a business uses a configured customer communication workflow.

Information handled

Depending on the workflow, information may include a customer's name, phone number, messages, product interest, location, budget and other details voluntarily provided during an enquiry.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, understand customer requirements, organise leads, maintain relevant business records, notify authorised staff and support requested follow-up.

Service providers and sharing

A configured workflow may process information through the WhatsApp Business Platform and other business tools selected for the service, such as AI or record-management systems. Information should be shared only as needed to operate the requested workflow and subject to the applicable service terms and permissions.

Retention and security

Information may be retained in the systems configured for a business. Access, retention and deletion should be managed according to the business's requirements, applicable law and relevant provider policies. Appropriate access controls should be used to protect stored information.

Your choices

Customers can contact the business they messaged to ask about their enquiry information or request assistance with their data. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and the business's responsibilities.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated as services and workflows change. For questions about a particular enquiry, please contact the relevant business through its usual customer support channel.

Service Information

Automation services are configured according to the agreed requirements of each business. Features, integrations and availability depend on the selected systems, permissions and applicable provider terms.

Automated or AI-assisted responses may occasionally be incomplete or inaccurate. Businesses should review important information and use human judgement where appropriate. No particular business result is guaranteed.

Use of WhatsApp and other integrated services remains subject to their respective terms, policies and technical requirements.
